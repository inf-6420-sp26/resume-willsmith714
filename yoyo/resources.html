Yo-Yoing sounds fun! Where do I start?

I would love to teach everyone individually how to yo-yo, but I do not have enough time to do that. That being said, there are many online resources and databases that contain everything you need to learn how to yo-yo. I'd recommend three places to start:

  1. YoTricks: I learned how to yo-yo using YoTricks. The YouTube channel contains everything a beginner should know how to do, including frequently asked questions and advanced tutorials on some more difficult tricks. Although their format is a bit outdated in how tricks are taught, the in depth tutorials are perfect for beginners! Check out their how to start yo-yoing video below!
  1. Yo-Yo Store Rewind: Rewind is arguably the largest yo-yo store in the world, carrying several hundred styles of yo-yos at any time. They also have one of the largest modern playlists for tricks, with over 1,000 tricks to learn from. They have tutorials for all levels of tricks from world famous yo-yo players in all different styles. Even better, they are wonderful people! They have an English and Japanese YouTube channel. The English channel contains less content, but is all in English. Their Japanese channel contains a huge amount of information, but is all in Japanese. With English subtitles provided by youTube, they are one of, if not the best resources to gain information from!
  • Rewind YouTube Channel (English)
  • Rewind YouTube Channel (Japanese)
  1. Skill Addicts: Something that most people do not know is that there is a huge yo-yo hub in Czechia. SLUSNY collaborated with Skill Addicts to create an app you can download for your phone that gives you tutorials, allows you to film yourself, and get real feedback from real people! It is a great place for beginners, and goes beyond yo-yos! There are also recommended yo-yos for beginners, which is always a nice resource to have!
  • Skill Addicts

Your First Yoyo

If you really want to try out yo-yoing, here are a few options you can consider:

  • Duncan Buttefly This cheap beginner yo-yo can do many tricks and does not break the bank!
  • C3 Speedaholic XX: If you are interested in modern competitive you-yoing, this is the yo-yo for you! A plastic body creates a strong durable option for both responsive and unresponsive yo-yoing.
  • Recess First Base: A really fun yo-yo for all skill levels. I love my First Base!
